## Full description

```
Nonograms (also known as Picross, Griddlers or Japanese crosswords) are relaxing logic puzzles: use the numbers next to each row and column to fill the grid and reveal a hidden picture.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Endless levels in three sizes: 5×5 warm-up, 10×10 classic and 15×15 expert. Every puzzle is generated to be solvable by pure logic — no guessing.
• Daily puzzle with a day streak.
• Picture gallery: 38 hand-drawn nonograms — heart, cat, house, owl, sailboat, panda and more.
• Comfortable controls: tap to fill, drag to fill a whole line, cross mode, undo, auto-crosses for finished lines.
• Hints when you are stuck, optional mistake checking.
• Stars, statistics, best times, dark theme, Russian and English.

100% OFFLINE
No internet needed. Progress is saved automatically and an unfinished puzzle can be resumed any time.
```
